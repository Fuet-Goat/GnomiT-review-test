  rule 'cascades never create rewards' is superseded as the TARGET design.
- Creation and activation are separate. A newly created power waits for the
  player's deliberate adjacent swap of THAT power. Birth, gravity, elapsed
  turns, neighbouring matches, and incidental adjacency do not auto-use it.

  example lets the player continue and choose when to move the power later.
- Newborn powers never auto-fuse into Black Hole just because they land together.
  Deliberate adjacent swap of two already-existing five-born powers remains
  the human's 5+5 Black Hole condition, including same families.
- Keep the power table: straight4->Volcano,straight5+->Storm,T/plus->Tornado,
  L->Landslide. Mineral remains distinct from family and birth tier.
