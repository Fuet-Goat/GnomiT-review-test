GNOMIT — SURFACE MINE / UNDERGROUND CHAPTER 1
19 October 2026 · DESIGN RECORD · NOT IMPLEMENTED

1. HUMAN DIRECTION RECORDED
The adventure descends into the earth. The opening shows a mine entrance
surrounded by a green surface world, vegetation and a small replaceable guide.
The guide initially points out where to go, explains play and introduces
new concepts with short cards and mini animations. Enter the mine to reach
the first underground chapter. The latest requested count is interpreted as
TEN playable sublevels PLUS a separate Boss: eleven boards in Chapter 1.
These are destinations within a chapter, not eleven surface buildings.

The user asked whether every level should introduce a new bonus. That is
an open design question, not an instruction already accepted as a rule.
Recommendation: one new idea at a time; alternate introductions with practice.
The concrete lesson schedule below is a proposal, not human-approved content.

2. SPATIAL HIERARCHY
Surface gateway -> Mine entrance -> Underground chapter map -> Level preview
-> explicit PLAY -> existing match3 board -> result -> underground chapter map.

The outdoor scene establishes the mine and story. Its visual focus is the
entrance, with foliage, rocks, timber, warm lamp and a small guide. It does
not contain the chapter's ten numbered nodes winding across the green lawn.
The current Blender diorama is an asset/pipeline proof, not the canonical
final layout. Reuse suitable mine/vegetation/rock assets; do not adopt its
entire outdoor boardwalk as the underground level journey.

The chapter map starts just below the entrance with daylight receding above,
exposed roots, soil and shallow stone, then becomes darker as the player
descends. A shared authored route defines the visible road and marker motion.
Playable mineral cells remain readable; world depth never darkens the board.

Longer story order: surface -> soil/roots and shallow mine -> deeper rocky
caves in the crust -> mantle much later. The mantle lies beneath the crust.
The fantasy Hollow Earth belongs to a later story arc, not this first slice.
These are game regions, not a claim that the first ten short puzzles span
the actual planet's geological thickness. No fixed metres-per-level yet.

3. FIRST VISIT
A. Establish the mine in a calm living surface shot at 432x960.
B. Small guide points to the entrance; one short English line:
   "Follow my lantern. Tap the mine entrance."
C. Tap the entrance -> short authored walk into the opening -> brief camera/
   lighting transition into the underground chapter. No walking through rock.
D. On the FIRST visit only, show Level 1's goals and PLAY over the chapter
   map automatically, saving an unnecessary second selection step.
E. PLAY starts the real existing board. No automatic match3 start, cost,
   reward, move or power just from touching/entering the mine.
F. A real win returns to the underground route, unlocks the next board and
   moves the small marker a short distance. Tap an unlocked node -> goals/
   PLAY immediately; later level selection never waits for avatar walking.

The guide walks as a short entrance introduction, not a new free-roam RPG.
His portrait, dialogue and animation references are swappable presentation
data, not part of Core, material recipes or completion prerequisites.

Returning players use Continue to resume their current underground node.
Do not replay the entrance walk/tutorial on every launch or after every win.
The surface stays accessible deliberately. Re-entering it is not required
between the chapter's levels. One-finger vertical map dragging remains.

4. TUTORIAL CARDS / MINI ANIMATIONS — PROPOSED BEHAVIOR
Each card teaches one idea with a tiny board example, a short line and a
visible Continue/Replay demo action. Illustrate both the creation pattern
and how the player deliberately swaps the resulting bonus to activate it.
Do not depend on the player reading a long paragraph while action continues.

Cards are presentation: pause live board input/resolution and movement time;
use a separate demo model so there is no live RNG, score, inventory, power,
reward or progress mutation. A preview animation is not a player-earned use.
Persist seen lesson IDs separately from wins and rewards. Replay explanation
remains available; returning players do not see already-seen cards forced
again. Persisting/renaming any such IDs needs defined save compatibility.

Reduced Motion: still before/after diagrams or instant steps, no forced
camera travel, looping sway or rapid flashes. Sound respects the game's
existing sound option. No modal nesting that strands the player or hides PLAY.
Demo completion alone never marks a level won. Boss contains learned ideas;
it does not introduce an unexplained power or require a rare Black Hole.

5. PROPOSED CHAPTER 1 LESSON PACING — NOT FINAL LEVEL DATA
01  Basic adjacent drag/swap and a match of three; a simple collection goal.
02  Practice matching/collection with fewer hints; no new power.
03  Introduce Volcano creation from four in a line, then deliberate use.
04  Practice Volcano; new power waits for the player's intentional swap.
05  Soil/gravity or another already-supported obstacle, with clear feedback.
06  Introduce Storm from five in a line; show outer-edge targets in the demo.
07  Practice Storm and the familiar objective; no additional power recipe.
08  Apply familiar skills without a forced tutorial interruption.
09  Prepare the runes using the known adjacent-match rule.
10  Charge the lantern / rehearse the learned Boss goals.
BOSS Crystal Guardian: combine learned collection/rune/light goals.

This schedule deliberately proposes only TWO new powers for the opening
chapter. Tornado, Landslide, Black Hole and a possible water/Tsunami lesson
can be introduced in later chapters, interleaved with practice. Existing
assets/rules are retained; delaying their lesson does not discard the work.
If the owner instead wants all powers in Chapter 1, treat that as a content
pacing change, not as something already decided by the two other bots.

Recipes remain material-independent: material/colour is separate from power
type; 4-line -> Volcano, 5-line -> Storm, T-five -> Tornado, L-five -> Landslide.
Black Hole stays a deliberate direct swap of two existing five-tier powers;
it is not triggered by adjacency, entry, a demonstration or an automatic fall.
Keep the latest human-approved cascade/waiting-power behavior when selecting
the real integration predecessor. Do not substitute older demo rules.
