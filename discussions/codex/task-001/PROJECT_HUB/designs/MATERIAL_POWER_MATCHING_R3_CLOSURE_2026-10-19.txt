COMMON FIRST-SLICE CONTRACT / CODEX ADOPTS THE PEER RECOMMENDATION
1. Keep straight4->Volcano;straight5+->Storm;T/plus->Tornado;L->Landslide.
   Keep mineral, power family, birth tier and immutable token ID separate.
   Existing shape priority remains the current prototype choice.
2. ALL-ORDINARY cascade matches may create rewards with the same recipe
   classifier. One reward per connected matched component, not per sub-window.
   Multiple independent components can create more than two rewards per turn.
3. Every created power waits across turns for deliberate adjacent player
   movement of THAT power. Birth, gravity, elapsed turns, nearby colour lines,
   and incidental adjacency never activate or auto-fuse it.
4. Initial PLAYER-made material matches can use a stationary waiting power as
   a mineral catalyst. It stays with the same ID, never fires or clears. New
   reward uses an ordinary matched anchor; do not overwrite the stationary power.
   For [R,R,V_R,R] formed by moving an ORDINARY ruby: keep V_R, retain one
   ordinary anchor as new Volcano, remove two ordinary rubies, no eruption.
5. During AUTOMATIC falling/refill matching, waiting powers are barriers to
   ordinary runs; [R,V_R,R] leaves all three pieces. No clearing neighbours
   around a protected power, including later turns. Material meaning is phase-
   specific and must be explained visually rather than sold as passive matching.
6. Deliberately swapping an actual power with an ordinary neighbour still
   activates it. If that same move creates a qualifying initial material match,
   resolve the old effect and one new reward atomically from the post-swap
   snapshot; a reserved new anchor survives and receives a DIFFERENT ability ID.
   If the moved power is not inside the material component, it still fires ONCE;
   do not suppress deliberate use merely because the OTHER endpoint made a match.
7. Direct registered power-pair swaps have precedence:4+4/4+5 keep their own
   combined effects;5+5->one Black Hole. Do not add a second colour reward to
   the same direct pair swap. Incidental same-material membership is not fusion.
8. Ordinary individual power attacks retain ordinary-only target eligibility;
   don't broaden is_ordinary just to implement mineral matching. Black Hole is
   the human-approved inert-swallow exception for other waiting powers.

MATERIAL ACCOUNTING — COMMON PEER PROPOSAL, NOT AN EXTRA HUMAN RULING
One materially removed token can count at most ONE stored mineral unit toward
the matching goal. A power never represents the original4/5 pieces as collectible
units. Stationary catalyst contributes ZERO because it survives. A retained
ordinary/new-power anchor contributes ZERO. If an old ABILITY retires at the new
anchor, its one material unit transfers to the new token: ZERO anchor collection,
even though the old effect fired. Elsewhere a genuinely removed old power may
contribute one stored material unit, never a guessed material or double award.
Codex applies this proposal uniformly to direct use and inert Black Hole removal,
including the two consumed sources; an unknown legacy material gives no fake goal.
This is stated explicitly for review, not attributed to the human's swallow answer.
One removed material instance, one goal signal; overlap with an attack never
counts twice. Matching clears use existing floor/rune rules once per wave;
retained anchors/catalysts take no fictitious terrain damage. Direct Black Hole
still preserves soil/runes; subsequent real ordinary cascades may affect them.

DEDUPLICATION MUST TRACK INSTANCES, NOT COORDINATES
Grok575 'set of ordinary cells remembered for the turn' needs this qualification:
freshly refilled tokens can legitimately form another four at the SAME indices.
Use actual participating material-instance IDs / wave occurrence plus a consumed
ledger. An unchanged already-resolved component cannot pay twice; genuinely new
tokens at reused coordinates CAN earn a new reward. A wave number alone cannot
turn an unchanged component into a new payment. Anchor conversion and clears
must commit once before the next scan, so the old ordinary shape ceases to exist.

   on drain entry, at most floor(N/3) productive drain waves plus a final scan.
   Process every legitimate4/5 reward during this drain; don't silently cap it.
D. Once the partial board has no ordinary3+ matches, fill only genuinely empty
   playable cells in a recorded deterministic order. Choose a normal mineral
   that creates no ordinary triple in ANY of the three windows per axis through
   that cell, checking [_,A,A], [A,_,A] and [A,A,_]. Left/up-only is insufficient.
   On one axis these overlapping windows forbid at most TWO distinct colours;
   both axes forbid at most FOUR. With the current SIX ordinary kinds, at least
   TWO valid choices remain. Choose deterministically / record exact RNG use.
   Keep existing settled gems and waiting powers unchanged; no retry loop or
   whole-board reroll is needed for colour safety. Do not move pieces again
   after this safe fill. Powers/holes/runes break ordinary match windows.
E. Final invariant: no ordinary automatic matches, all earned waiting power
   identities preserved, each legitimate recipe/goal paid once, one player move.
   A separate genuine no-move check may use the existing bounded ordinary-only
   shuffle policy, preserving powers; reaching the cascade guard alone must not
   invoke it again. Record recovery as real Core events for normal/skip/reduced.
