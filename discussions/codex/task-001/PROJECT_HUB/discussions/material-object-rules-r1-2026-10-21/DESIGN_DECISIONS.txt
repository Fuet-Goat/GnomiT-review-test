PROPOSED — NOT_IMPLEMENTED — 21.10.2026

Approved direction: distinct damage conditions for both minerals and obstacles,
visible differentiation, and two-page mini explanations at first introduction.

Pending design choice: protected variants vs special rules on every base material.
Do not implement a different answer by assuming that a colour now means a power.

Before one conditional piece is activated, define:
- movement/fall policy and whether it participates in matching while protected;
- exact valid damage sources, HP, and what happens when protection reaches zero;
- whether the revealed mineral is collected now or remains as an ordinary piece;
- whether it counts in 4/5/T/L, and where a new reward may safely be anchored;
- one-hit Black Hole behavior on protection/mineral/floor without double damage;
- interaction with waiting powers and cascades, including no infinite re-match;
- scoring once per actually collected instance, not once per visual fragment;
- introduction only when the needed action/power is available and demonstrated.

One candidate example, not an approved recipe:
Armoured diamond visibly wears a stone casing. A demonstrated valid hit cracks
it; the next valid hit removes the protection. Decide separately whether the
diamond remains, contributes to matches while protected, and what hits are valid.
Do not silently turn this incomplete example into a level-generation rule.

Visual principles:
Crystals may fracture into faceted shards; ore crumbles into dense chunks;
pearl may split/shed a smooth shell; soil sheds low dust; rune stone splits
around its engraving. Those animations do not by themselves define damage rules.
One existing break cue per resolution wave/event remains; no per-cell sound stack.
Partial damage must look different from final destruction and retain readable HP.
Art/shapes/powers/tutorial ids must be registered with provenance and metadata.

Acceptance for the first real prototype:
- At432x960 the special variant is recognizable before the player touches it.
- Lesson correctly demonstrates one accepted and one rejected action if needed.
- Each accepted event damages that instance at most once; no duplicate reward.
- Partial-hit and final-break are visibly distinct; reduced motion same result.
- Existing ordinary matches, reward recipes, objectives and save remain compatible.
- Real native playtest plus human acceptance before more variants are introduced.
