VISUAL RESULT: HOLD / REPAIR_NOT_ACHIEVED.
R4 form and M2 finish remain human-accepted. The revised native is a diagnostic,
not an accepted Rig R2. Actual front and matched three-quarter PNGs were viewed.
Cuff pinching improved, but an unnatural underarm web and a small shoulder-cap
silhouette dimple remain. Better edge ratios or zero sampled BVH candidates do
not satisfy the human no-rubber/no-pinching criteria.
Weight-only A/B/C, helper D/E and native CorrectiveSmooth F were measured.
B/C/D/F retained visible defects; E and unconstrained pose optimisation created
new local strain. F was not adopted and is not in the held final native.
The held candidate uses one permitted cap support bone plus bounded multi-pose
strain/curvature optimisation, with original animation and source meshes kept.
No new blink, animation amplitude reduction, game changes or integration.
Next proposal is local sleeve/underarm topology/deformation-flow work while
preserving the accepted outer rest appearance; NOT a redesign of the gnome.
That proposed topology work has NOT been executed or approved by this report.
