NATIVE WORLD AND GUIDE
V3 is an isolated derivative of the preserved V2 art-preview project.
Geometry, articulated guide pivots, animation keys and materials are authored
through the included Blender Python sources. No external model pack, downloaded
character, raster landscape used as geometry or new paid service was introduced.
Authoring: existing Blender 5.2.2 LTS. Runtime: existing Godot 4.7.2 Forward+.
No Unity, Maya or newly installed software was used for this V3 pass.

The oak maps and new meadow map are mathematical procedural textures produced
by the included sources. Four oak images plus one meadow image are packed in
the .blend and exported through GLB. The guide uses native geometry/materials,
not an image cutout. Source scripts expose construction and animation; this is
not a statement of exclusive copyright or legal clearance. The separate rights
audit and applicable tool/input licences still apply.

V3 additions include camera framing, 0.11 m board thickness, honey oak colour,
local grass/moss detail, 44 native tufts, interior rail props, cloud drift,
contained flame animation and separate guide head/blink groups. Guide boots and
terrain roots remain fixed in the measured native loop.
The six-second loop has 144 intervals between frame 1 and frame 145 at 24 fps.
Export uses export_anim_slide_to_zero=True; Godot import uses 24 fps.
verification/GUIDE_RUNTIME_TIMELINE_BEFORE_FIX.json retains the failed timing
check. Final imported-animation receipt: renders/GUIDE_RUNTIME_AUDIT.json
(51 checks, no failures).
