OFFICIAL SOURCES — checked during initial audit, client date 2026-10-19
Summaries only. No source is a license for an unrelated third-party recording.

Blender License
https://www.blender.org/about/license/
Direct page and manual fetch errored; official indexed license/manual text reviewed:
https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/getting_started/about/license.html
GPL applies to Blender itself; artwork created with it does not inherit GPL merely
because of the tool. Third-party inputs and add-ons need separate review.

Audacity FAQ: About Audacity
https://manual.audacityteam.org/man/faq_about_audacity.html
Audacity is free for personal/commercial/educational use. Tool availability does
not clear a downloaded sample. Redistributing the application is a separate issue.

Godot: Complying with licenses
https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html
Godot MIT and bundled third-party notices need appropriate inclusion when
redistributing the engine; game assets/source keep their separate licenses.

SIL Open Font License FAQ
https://openfontlicense.org/ofl-faq/
OFL fonts can be bundled in games, including commercial games, with their notice
and license obligations; the game need not be open-sourced because of the font.
Actual local font metadata and local OFL text are in evidence/.

Epic Games Fan Content Policy
https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/fan-art-policy
This policy is centered on noncommercial fan content, with limited exceptions.
It is not a general grant for Epic game recordings in a commercial third-party
game. Application to the supplied MP3 is conditional: exact authorship/content
was not independently established by its filename.

OpenAI Europe Terms of Use
https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/
Reviewed in this audit for generated-media provenance: allocation of output
between user and OpenAI does not clear third-party references; the user remains
responsible for input rights. No assertion of exclusivity or copyright validity
for every AI image is made here. Historical provider terms at generation time
and each reference input remain evidence to retain.

All descriptions are operational audit guidance for the reviewed materials,
