2. Corridor hit-test comfort (undemonstrated edge case)
   Evidence: HALF_WIDTH=38 source pixels; closest() used in _end_pointer. Screenshots show successful taps, but no near-miss or finger-size testing.
   Minimal change: optionally widen HALF_WIDTH slightly or add soft visual feedback on near-miss.

3. Arrival panel layering (minor visual)
   Evidence: 04_ARRIVAL_PLAY.png and 08_LEVEL_2_ARRIVAL.png show panel; arrival_button positioned at bottom. Potential overlap with badge depending on scroll.

- Defect: none that break the expected contract (drag/stop, tap-to-walk, no teleport, Play-only start, unlock persistence).
