First-probe clarity rule: a cascade may clear and fall, but it does not create a tornado, a volcano, or a black hole. Only the match made by the player's own swap can create a tornado or volcano. The black-hole resolution refills with ordinary gems only and creates nothing. At most one black hole activation per level attempt. The same resolution cannot start another one. The next player move is a new transaction.

The volcano roll is taken once from the level seed and stored in the gameplay event. Playback, skip, pause and reduced motion do not roll again.

C. BLACK HOLE — THREE CONDITIONS

Ranked. No measured percentages.

1. Recommended. Tornado x Volcano contact.
The player swaps an existing tornado with an orthogonally adjacent volcano. Example on a 0-based 7x8 board: Tornado-H at (2,3), Volcano at (3,3). The swap consumes both and opens the hole on that contact. Ordinary gems clear. The hole mask, floor bits and runes stay. The refill is ordinary gems and contains no second hole.
Skill: the player made both shapes earlier and then moved them together. Luck: the board has to offer both a 4-line and an L/T before they can be joined. Rarity: high, but it uses only pieces the player already understands. Clarity: the two icons touch; no counter and no new button. Frustration: a player can hold one piece for a while. That is acceptable because the hole is optional. HUD: none. Goal risk: low if the sweep does not clear soil or runes. This is not the rejected "two identical cores" rule. The two pieces have different, already taught effects, and the swap replaces both effects instead of stacking them.

2. Fallback only. Visible two-mark charge, no Arm button.
In one level attempt, one fired tornado and one fired volcano light two small pips already sitting in the goal row. The next intentional tornado or volcano activation is replaced by the hole. Cascades do not add pips. The hole does not relight them. One use per attempt.
Example: move 5 fires Tornado-H, move 8 fires a volcano, move 9 the player swaps a new tornado and the hole fires instead of the line.
Skill: choosing to spend both powers. Luck: rolling both shapes. Clarity: good if the pips are only two marks. Frustration: short or hole-heavy levels may never offer both. HUD: two pips, which I would accept only if contact swaps prove too rare. I do not want a separate Arm button on a 432-wide screen.

3. Not recommended as the first condition. Codex chain A.
Before the move, Tornado-H, Tornado-V and a volcano all exist. The player fires H so that its row hits V, V's column hits the volcano, and the volcano cell becomes the hole instead of erupting. Example: H=(1,1), V=(4,1), Volcano=(4,4).
Skill: very high geometry. Luck: very high, because all three must already exist and sit on one deterministic path. Clarity: the chain is readable if each hit is shown. Frustration: highest for a casual player, especially across holes. HUD: none. I would keep this only as a later optional flourish, not as the way the hole is introduced.

The old "5 in a line makes a core; two adjacent cores make the hole" stays a rejected comparison. A lone core is a dead piece, and the human already asked for a more special condition.
