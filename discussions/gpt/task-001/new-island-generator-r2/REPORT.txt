ONE R2 REVISION
1. BOARDWALK: smoother four-segment route, jointly revised spacing/depth search.
The previous solver could satisfy overlap by shrinking boards into thin fans.
R2 keeps SAT, rejects depth below 66% of centre spacing or 0.08m, and searches
bounded candidate counts. It also rejects raw outer seams above 0.09m and a
bevel-adjusted separation bound above 0.106m. These are TARGET LIMITS in the
new code, not measured achieved seams. New centres/tangents must satisfy all
conditions or the build reports failure; no permissive fallback. Full-width
rectangular boards remain. The corrected underdeck winding is retained verbatim;
its material becomes honey wood, with an 0.008m recess. Colour is a supporting
change, not the geometric fix. Level anchors and editable centreline remain.

2. ISLAND/CAMERA: deeper tapered earthen profile, 2.45m proposed earth_depth
instead of 1.28m; rounded shoulder, broader middle body, sculpted broad vertical
rhythm, planar closed underside. A modest grass dome replaces the flat top.
The proposed 43-degree camera elevation replaces 53 degrees; the existing
fit-to-all-evaluated-geometry method still frames crowns and the earth mass.
A slightly broader/longer footprint leaves quiet lawns around the new route.
Actual projected proportions, visibility and similarity remain to be rendered.

3. LANDMARKS/COLOUR: mine arch becomes nine rounded-edge shaped stone blocks
with broad faces, not eleven near-identical ellipsoids. The original hollow
tunnel, frame, braces, lantern and object separation are kept. Stone colours
are darker neutral warm gray. Cottage wall and door are taller; roof/spots/lip
are moved together so the cream wall can be read. Tree crowns are more
asymmetric and fuller around the main mass; sparse leaf tips and existing

is separated from neutral ambient illumination; this new node arrangement

and 18 tuft targets instead of 18/22, existing bounded rejection and quiet zones.
