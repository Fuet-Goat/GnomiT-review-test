2. ONE PROPOSED SOLUTION
A fresh elongated grassy island with a rounded turf lip and warm earthen cut,
a honey-coloured wide S boardwalk with two main bends and a short mine approach,
an upper-right open mine, a left red mushroom cottage and five rich rounded trees.
The new numeric layout is hand-authored from the primary middle-panel composition.
It is NOT a copy of the old _xy route, old camera, or old world mesh.
Portrait framing fits evaluated geometry bounds, including tree crowns.
The longer island is intentional: fit to 432x960 without cropping side crowns.

The new silhouette is proposed with 3.55m half-width and 9.25m half-length;
these are chosen build inputs, NOT dimensions measured from the reference.
Camera angle, lengths, palette, 18 small stones, six flower groups and three
quiet meadows are initial artistic choices, NOT measured similarity/density claims.
Approximately one-third fewer decorations in the brief is treated qualitatively.
Mine stones are structural and are not reduced with the small-stone scatter.

Road: new cubic Bezier control points; uniform arc-length placement; rectangular
constant-width boards with adaptively shortened depth at bends. A 2D separating-axis
check prevents board footprint intersections. A continuous, recessed timber deck
fills the seams. Raised edge stringers and small rounded posts remain separate.
Road_Centerline has editable curve geometry plus sample points in metadata.
Level_1_Anchor lies on the lower path; Level_2_Anchor is at the entrance.
There are no visible numbers, UI panels, crowns, lettering or a gnome in this stage.

Mine: actual open thick tunnel walls and ceiling, separate rounded arch stones,
wooden frame, threshold planks and braces. A dark distant interior end is geometry,
not a front-facing black image or a flattened reference. Brass lantern cage and
emissive warm core are separate editable objects. Its local light is restrained.

Trees: shaped tapered trunk, flaring roots, branches, five overlapping soft crown
lobes, a few broad tip leaves and two small canopy daisies per tree. Cottage uses
native lathed wall/cap, raised white spots and door/window geometry. All objects
have distinct names and are organised in the ten required collections.

Materials: restrained portable Principled PBR. Analytic honey-wood and endgrain
PNG maps are generated INSIDE future Blender execution using standard-library
array/math, written to TEXTURES, and packed. No external maps, HDRI or service.
Grass, foliage, earth and stone use simple PBR colours with broad shape/shadow
variation. No live procedural shader is relied on for faithful GLB material transfer.
