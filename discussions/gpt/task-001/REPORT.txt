PRIORITY 1 — CONFUSING SECOND "PLAY" (CONCRETE UX MISLABEL / DUPLICATE ACTION)
Evidence: media/current/08_LEVEL_2_ARRIVAL.png shows a dimmed footer "Play Level 2" under the real panel Play. slice_map_view.gd:_sync_controls makes arrival_button visible and labels it "Play Level 2"; _open_arrived_level emits arrived_at_level, thus reopening the preview, rather than starting match-3. The panel's green Play calls pre_level_view.gd:request_play, which is the actual play action. _arrive() already opens the panel automatically.
Minimal correction: Remove/hide the redundant footer Play action when this First Bend flow is active; allow the level badge to reopen the panel after it is closed. Preserve the sole green Play in the panel and its existing guards. Do not change unrelated levels.
Visible acceptance at 432x960: each Level 1/2 arrival opens one goal/reward panel with exactly one Play call to action; dismissing and re-tapping the level reopens it; the board starts only when the panel's Play is used, once.

PRIORITY 2 — FIRST-BEND GAIT AND GROUNDING (ART-QUALITY FINDING, NOT A PROVEN GAMEPLAY DEFECT)
Evidence: slice_map_view.gd:_render_avatar selects [0,1,2,1] with a three-pose cycle in either direction; a single front/back pivot of approximately (0.46,0.975)/(0.46,0.965) is applied regardless of the shoe configuration of each frame. A separate procedural ContactShadow exists, which is an improvement over alpha10. The atlas has six generated frames. In 02_WALK.png and 09_RETURN_UP.png, feet, proportions and ground contact still look less convincing than the detailed road and lighting. A still image cannot prove temporal foot sliding.
Minimal correction: Normalize visible foot positions and scale per frame, tune the shadow width/opacity and movement cadence, and review a complete short forward-and-reverse loop before commissioning more sprites. Keep the same gnome design and map.
Visible acceptance at 432x960: a slow recorded loop across the bend in both directions keeps the planted shoe and shadow connected to the same road surface without visible hopping or sliding; size and lighting read as one storybook scene. Human judgement still required.

PRIORITY 3 — LOCKED ROAD HAS WEAK VISUAL FEEDBACK (PRESENTATION PREFERENCE / USABILITY RISK)
Evidence: 01_START.png shows a continuous wooden path toward a locked Level 2 badge. Runtime access IS blocked in code via accessible_s() and the hint path; this is not a bypass or broken unlock. 07_UNLOCKED_BEND.png communicates progression mostly through number color/stars, not a visibly opened passage.
Minimal correction: Add one restrained original storybook obstacle or sign at the Level 1 boundary (e.g. wood rope/lock), visible only while entry is unbeaten; remove/fold it back after a legitimate Level 1 win. No new persistent progression state or excessive particles.
Visible acceptance at 432x960: before winning, players can recognize the passage is closed and cannot walk past it; after winning, the obstacle visibly opens and the same route can be traversed without altering stars/claimed/Light logic.

Important scoped limitation, NOT a fourth finding: New navigation is only authored for the first bend. Outside it, slice_map_view.gd falls back to the older alpha10 direct entry. The prototype save is separate from the main game's save. Both must be revisited before any production rollout, but neither is a defect against this deliberately narrow slice contract.
